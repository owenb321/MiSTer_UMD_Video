# What you need

| | |
|---|---|
| **MiSTer** | A DE10-Nano running MiSTer. No add-on boards are required; the core uses the on-board DDR3 only. |
| **Images** | A `.iso` image of a UMD Video disc, made from a disc you own, or a single clip's `.mps` stream file. See [UMD images](images.md). |
| **Display** | Any HDMI display, or an analog CRT: native 480i engages from `MiSTer.ini` like other cores (see [Analog and CRT output](../video/analog-crt.md)). |

No UMD drive exists for a PC or a MiSTer, but a PSP with custom firmware can share the disc in
its drive over USB, and the core plays it ([Playing a real UMD](psp-disc.md)).
