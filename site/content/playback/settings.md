# Settings

The core's OSD options. Every option the core defines is listed here; the list is checked
against the core's own definition on every build.

## Main page

| Option | Values | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Video Output | Auto, Interlaced, Progressive | Auto | The raster the core sends to HDMI and the analog output alike. **Auto** chooses **Interlaced** (480i) when `MiSTer.ini` sets up an analog display (composite sync, YPbPr or sync-on-green without `vga_scaler`, or `direct_video`; S-Video and composite users choose Interlaced themselves), and **Progressive** (480p) otherwise. Auto settles while nothing is playing; a forced choice applies at once. See [Analog and CRT output](../video/analog-crt.md). |
| Aspect Ratio | Auto, 4:3, 16:9, Zoom | Auto | The aspect presented to the scaler. **Auto** shows a loaded video in the shape its stream declares (widescreen titles at 16:9, 4:3 titles at 4:3; a stream that declares nothing is shown at 16:9, which almost every UMD Video title is) and fits the idle screen to the display. **4:3** and **16:9** force the shape. **Zoom** is for a widescreen film stored letterboxed in a 4:3 picture, which a 16:9 television otherwise shows with black on all four sides: it shows the middle three quarters of a 4:3 picture's height at 16:9, filling the screen with the film at its true shape. Zoom crops the top and bottom of every 4:3 title while it is selected, so switch back to Auto for a title that fills its 4:3 picture. Widescreen titles, the disc's menus, a stopped disc and the interlaced (480i) output are shown as in Auto. Subtitles are moved up into the zoomed picture, never cut. See [Video output](../video/output.md#letterboxed-43-titles-zoom). |
| Deinterlace | Weave, Bob, Blend | Weave | Shown on the progressive (480p) output only. How a picture that was recorded as two interlaced fields is shown on the progressive output. **Weave** shows both fields together at full resolution; moving edges can comb. **Bob** shows each field on its own at the full field rate, filling the missing lines from the ones above and below: smooth motion, half the vertical detail. **Blend** mixes the two fields: no combing and no flicker, with a soft ghost on fast motion. Film is shown whole whatever the setting. Progressive pictures are never altered. Film stored as interlaced fields (most European imports and animated titles) is recognised by its 3:2 rhythm after a second or two of motion; until then, and on true interlaced video, this setting applies ([Compatibility](../reference/compatibility.md#video)). A change takes effect immediately. |
| HDMI Deinterlace | Weave, Bob | Weave | Shown on the interlaced (480i) output only, in place of Deinterlace. The analog output always carries true fields; this chooses how the MiSTer's HDMI scaler turns them into a progressive picture. **Weave** joins the two fields: full detail, combing on motion in interlaced titles. **Bob** shows each field on its own: no combing, a little vertical shimmer on fine detail. The scaler has no Blend. |
| Analog Aspect | Auto, Fit, Letterbox, Crop | Auto | Shown on the interlaced (480i) output only. How a widescreen (16:9) title fits a 4:3 CRT. **Auto** letterboxes 16:9 titles and shows 4:3 titles as they are. **Fit** never changes the picture (right for a widescreen television set to 16:9). **Letterbox** shows the whole picture at its true shape with black bars above and below. **Crop** fills the screen with the centre of the picture, its sides cut off. See [Analog and CRT output](../video/analog-crt.md#widescreen-titles-on-a-43-television). A disc's menus are fitted too: Auto letterboxes them ([Disc menus](disc-menus.md#on-a-43-crt)). |
| Analog Dither | Off, On | Off | For the analog output on an I/O board whose video DAC has 6 bits a colour (the DE10-Nano's standard analog I/O board): such a DAC shows smooth dark gradients (a sky, a fade, a dim scene) as visible bands. **On** adds a fine pattern that the television blurs away, so the gradients come out smooth. Leave it **Off** on a board with a full 8-bit analog DAC (a SuperStation, a 24-bit analog board): it has no bands to remove. HDMI is never affected. A change takes effect immediately. See [Analog and CRT output](../video/analog-crt.md#banding-in-dark-scenes-analog-dither). |
| Menu Language | English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Dutch, Japanese | English | The language of a disc's own menus, for discs that carry their menus in several languages (as the PSP's **UMD Menu Language** video setting). A disc without the chosen language opens its English menu, else the one it has. Needs the UMD Main ([Disc menus](disc-menus.md)). |
| Disc Menus | On, Off | On | Shown only with the UMD Main installed, which runs the disc menus. **On** starts a disc the way a PSP does, through its own menus. **Off** plays the disc's main title straight away, as the core does with the stock Main, continuing from where you last left it ([Going on where you left off](disc-menus.md#going-on-where-you-left-off)). Takes effect the next time an image is loaded. See [Disc menus](disc-menus.md). |
| Reset | | | Unloads the image and returns to the idle screen. Nothing plays until you load an image again. |

## Saved settings

Settings are saved to `config/UMD_v1.CFG` on the SD card. If a future version changes the
option layout the file name's version number is bumped and every setting returns to its
default once.
