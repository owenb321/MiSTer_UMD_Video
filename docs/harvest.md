# Harvest table: what transfers from MiSTer_DVD, and when

MiSTer_DVD (`../MiSTer_DVD`, the sibling checkout) is the source of most of this core's
plumbing. Its modules are NOT copied speculatively: each is brought over in the roadmap
phase that wires it, with the surgery listed here, and gets its bench ported in the same
change (a bench copied without its module is dead weight; a module without its bench is
a MiSTer_DVD lesson).

Classification: **V** verbatim (path/header edits only) · **S** surgery (named) ·
**T** template (write a new module in its shape) · **X** do not copy.
